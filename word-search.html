WORD SEARCH

Hiding some words…

How to play Word Search

  1. Every word in the list is hidden somewhere in the grid. Find one and it gets crossed off.
  2. Tap the first letter, then tap the last letter. No dragging — dragging on a phone scrolls the page instead, which is why most word searches are annoying on one.
  3. Words run in straight lines. On Easy that means across and down only; on Standard and Tricky they can also go diagonally and backwards.

An example

The theme is Fruit and one of the words is MANGO. You spot an M in the third row and think you can see the rest running down-right. Tap the M, then tap the O five squares along that diagonal — if the letters between them spell MANGO, the whole line turns green and the word is crossed off the list. Tap the wrong square and nothing is lost; just tap again.

On your own · 3–10 minutes · suggested from age 6 on Easy · free, no sign-up, plays in any browser.
