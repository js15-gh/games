How to play Tonight's Lobby

  1. Start a lobby and share the one link with everyone playing.
  2. Add everybody's name once. Whoever starts it holds the chair and picks the games.
  3. Every game you open puts the same people straight into their seats, and the evening's scores collect in one place.

An example

You add Asha, Bilal and Chetan, then tap The Outsider. It opens with all three already seated — no code to read out, no names to type. When it finishes, the result appears back in the lobby and you tap the next game.

Free, no accounts, nothing to install. Works in any phone browser.
