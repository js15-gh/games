MATHS SPRINT

How to play Maths Sprint

  1. Pick a level and a length, then answer as many as you can before the clock runs out.
  2. Three right in a row and the numbers get bigger; one wrong and they ease off. You find your own ceiling.
  3. At the end it shows every question you got wrong next to the right answer.

An example

You choose Standard and 60 seconds. 7 × 8 appears. You tap 5, then 6, then Answer — right, and your streak goes to three, so the next questions grow. Getting one wrong costs nothing but the seconds it took; the clock is the only pressure here.

On your own · about 1 minutes · suggested from age 8 · free, no sign-up, plays in any browser.
