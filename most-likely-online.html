MOST LIKELY TO

Reading the room…

How to play Most Likely To

  1. A prompt appears and everyone votes for somebody — including themselves.
  2. Votes stay hidden until the last one is in, so nobody follows the room.
  3. Then it all comes out at once and whoever got the most takes the card.

An example

The prompt is most likely to talk their way out of a parking ticket. Three of you pick Bilal, one picks Asha. It reveals together: Bilal 3, Asha 1. Bilal takes the card and has to sit there while everybody explains why.

3–16 players · about 15–30 minutes · suggested from age 8 · free, no sign-up, plays in any browser.
