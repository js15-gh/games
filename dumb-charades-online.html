Rolling the titles…

How to play Bollywood Dumb Charades

  1. Split into two teams. The opposing team chooses the film — they type it on their own phones.
  2. It appears on the actor's phone only. Nobody leaves the room and nobody whispers.
  3. Act it out. Your team shouts guesses against the clock.

An example

Team B types Sholay. It appears on Asha's screen and nowhere else. She starts acting; her team shouts. If they get it inside the time, Team A scores — and Team B never had to send anyone out of the room to keep the film secret.

4–20 players · about 20–40 minutes · suggested from age 8 · free, no sign-up, plays in any browser.
