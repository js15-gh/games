THIS OR THAT

Reading the room…

How to play This or That

  1. Each round one person is the subject. They answer a question on their own phone, where nobody can see it.
  2. Everybody else guesses what they said.
  3. A point for each guess that matches. The subject scores nothing — it is not their round to win.

An example

Asha is the subject and the question is Cinema or Sofa. She taps Cinema, unseen. Bilal guesses Cinema and takes a point; Chetan guesses Sofa and takes none. It looks like a poll and turns into an argument about who actually knows Asha.

3–16 players · about 15–25 minutes · suggested from age 8 · free, no sign-up, plays in any browser.
