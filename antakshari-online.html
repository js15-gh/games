Tuning up…

How to play Antakshari

  1. Two to four teams. The app holds the letter you must sing from, so nobody argues about it.
  2. Sing a song starting with that letter, then type the title in.
  3. If it has already been sung tonight the app says so immediately, and the last letter of your song becomes the next team's letter.

An example

The previous song ended on म (ma). Your team sings something beginning with it and types the title. The app checks it against every song used tonight, scores it, and hands the next team the letter your song ended on — which is the bookkeeping that usually ruins Antakshari at about song forty.

4–16 players · about Any length minutes · suggested from age 6 · free, no sign-up, plays in any browser.
