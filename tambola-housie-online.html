Printing the tickets…

How to play Tambola (Housie)

  1. One person is the caller; everyone else gets a real three-by-nine ticket on their own phone.
  2. The caller taps numbers as they come out. Any number on your ticket fills itself in — no pens, no missed calls.
  3. Claim a prize and the app checks it against the numbers actually called, not against what you tapped.

An example

The caller taps 47. It is on your ticket, so it fills in. Two calls later your top row is complete and you tap Top Line. The app checks all five numbers against what has genuinely been called and awards it — so a wrong claim is caught before it becomes an argument.

2–30 players · about 20–40 minutes · suggested from age 6 · free, no sign-up, plays in any browser.
