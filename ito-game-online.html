Threading the needle…

How to play Ito

  1. Everyone is dealt a secret number from 1 to 100. You can see only your own.
  2. A theme appears — how frightening, how expensive, how loud. Describe your number as a thing on that scale, without saying any number.
  3. Then play the cards in ascending order. Get the order wrong and the thread snaps.

An example

The theme is how frightening. You are holding 73, so you say “a wasp in the car”. Someone holding 12 says “a slightly cold bath”. Now the table has to decide who goes first — and if 73 is played before 12, you lose a life.

2–10 players · about 15–30 minutes · suggested from age 9 · free, no sign-up, plays in any browser.
