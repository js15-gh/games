How to play Daily Puzzle

  1. Sixteen words hide four groups of four. Find a group and it locks in.
  2. Four wrong guesses and the puzzle keeps its secrets.
  3. Everyone gets the same puzzle, and a new one arrives at midnight.

An example

Among the sixteen you spot Ladoo, Jalebi, Barfi and Peda. You tap all four and press Submit — they lock in as Indian sweets and leave twelve words behind. Get four wrong and it stops; but no word ever honestly belongs to two of the day's groups, so if it feels like it does, one of them is the trap.

On your own · about 5 minutes · suggested from age 9 · free, no sign-up, plays in any browser.
