WEREWOLF · MAFIA · TRAITORS

Reading the room…

How to play Werewolf, Mafia or Traitors

  1. Everyone joins on their own phone and taps their own name. Tap the face-down card and it tells you, privately, whether you are one of the villagers or one of the wolves — and hands you any special token you have been given.
  2. Night. One phone reads the instructions aloud: everyone closes their eyes and keeps tapping their phone, so the room cannot hear who is doing something. The wolves choose their victim on their own screens. Nobody else's screen shows anything unusual.
  3. Morning. Only the person who was taken sees it — their screen turns, and they hold it up. Everyone else finds out by looking at them.
  4. Day. Argue. Then everyone votes at once, secretly, and the votes are revealed together so nobody follows the room.
  5. The village wins when every wolf is gone. The wolves win the moment they equal the villagers in number.

An example

Eight play, with two wolves. On the first night the wolves both tap Priya. But Priya was dealt the shield, so in the morning everyone's screen says the night passed without a death — and Priya alone is told she was attacked and survived. She now knows something nobody else does, and has to decide whether saying so makes her safe or makes her the next target.

5–20 players · 20–45 minutes · suggested from age 12 · free, no sign-up, and nobody has to sit out to moderate.
