FIBBERS

Reading the room…

How to play Fibbers

  1. Slide one of your cards face down to somebody and tell them what it is. You do not have to be telling the truth.
  2. They can call it — true or false. Right, and the card goes back to you, face up. Wrong, and they keep it.
  3. Or they can look at it and pass it on to somebody who has not seen it, saying whatever they like.

An example

You pass a card to Bilal and say “this is a crow”. It is actually a monkey. Bilal peeks, does not fancy it, and passes it to Chetan saying “snake”. Chetan calls it a lie — correct — so Bilal keeps the monkey. Four of one kind and you lose.

3–8 players · about 20–30 minutes · suggested from age 8 · free, no sign-up, plays in any browser.
